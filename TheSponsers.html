Project Sponsors & Partners



The UFV Aerospace & Flight Centre combines funding, university leadership, airport participation, and a proposed aviation industry partnership. Each partner plays their role in this project and helps make everything possible.

Government of Canada - Primary Financial Sponsor



The Government of Canada will be providing the $100 million grant that makes the project possible. This funding will support the proposed facilities, aircraft fleet, simulators, staffing and licensing activities, student bursary endowment, software, and transportation services. The government’s interest is an amazing use of public funding to improve student access to aviation education, develop and train skilled pilots, and support innovation. The project team will provide financial reports and progress updates demonstrating how the funding is being used and what benefits are being delivered.

University of the Fraser Valley - Institutional Sponsor & Project Owner



UFV is the institutional sponsor, owner, and operator of the centre. The university will provide leadership, coordinate the King Road campus renovations, use the centre with its academic programs, and oversee the transition into ongoing operations. UFV’s role includes coordinating aviation and faculty, supporting staffing and curriculum planning, overseeing student services, and administering the proposed bursary endowment. The main goals are improving education, building futures and careers, reducing financial barriers, expanding practical learning opportunities, and maintaining a financially sustainable centre.

Abbotsford International Airport and the City of Abbotsford - Partners



YXX and the City of Abbotsford are essential partners in the proposed airport project. Their partnership would support the ground lease arrangement, coordination of the hangar development, and compatibility with airport operations and project requirements. The project team would work with these partners on site access, construction coordination, infrastructure needs, and operational arrangements. Their interest includes safe airport operations, appropriate land use, local employment, and the development of aviation education in Abbotsford. Kevin Renso will be the project’s Chacha and decide whether the team receives the contract. He evaluates whether the proposal offers sufficient student benefit, value for money, and a realistic and well-developed delivery plan. Kevin will lead the Change Control Board, review the major kill points, and approves significant changes to scope, schedule, or funding if needed. The project team will provide regular progress reports, cost forecasts, and explanations of major risks so that decisions are supported by evidence.

Boeing - Academic Research and Career Development Partner



Boeing is a academic research and career development partner for the centre at UFV. The project aims to establish an agreement with Boeing’s Everett and Vancouver operations to support aviation research, flight-data projects, and career development opportunities for students. Boeing’s role would include providing industry knowledge, supporting student research projects, and help connect classroom learning with the aviation industry. The main goals are expanding practical learning an opportunities, building students’ skills, and preparing aviation students for future careers. Boeing’s responsibilities will be outlined in the agreement. Funding, aircraft donations, and graduate employment are not guaranteed. The project team will communicate regularly with sponsors and partners through progress reports, financial updates, and meetings at important project stages
