What We Are Building

Every dollar of our $96.5M capital plan is locked to six concrete, measurable work packages:

YXX Airport Campus & Hangar ($28.0M)

Location: Abbotsford International Airport (YXX Tarmac)

What’s Included:

A 50-year municipal tarmac ground lease4 and turnkey construction of a 45,000 sq. ft. LEED-Gold Aviation Complex5.

Includes climate-controlled hangar bays

6 high-voltage electric aircraft fast-chargers

certified maintenance workshops

4 smart classrooms,

flight dispatch

UFV Student Lounge.

Next-Gen Sustainable Fleet ($25.0M)

Capacity: 25 Factory-New Training Aircraft

What’s Included:

15x Cessna 172S Skyhawks (Garmin G1000 NXi glass cockpits) for primary flight training6.

5x Diamond DA42-VI Twin Stars for commercial multi-engine IFR ratings7

5x Pipistrel Velis Electro zero-emission electric trainers + spare battery packs and tooling8,9.

Staffing, Licensing & Boeing MOU ($14.0M)

Team Size: 38 38 Full-Time Aviation & Support Staff

What’s Included:

Full Transport Canada Approved Training Organisation (ATO) certification

An academic research & pilot pipeline partnership with Boeing (Everett, WA & Vancouver)

3 years of funded onboarding for 1 Chief Flight Instructor, 20 Flight Instructors, 6 Aircraft Maintenance Engineers (AMEs), and support staff10,11.



King Road Simulator & CIS Wing ($12.0M)

Location: UFV Main Campus (Abbotsford)

What’s Included:

Structural and acoustic renovation of a dedicated main-campus wing housing 3 CAE/Frasca Level-D Full Motion Simulators

15 VR flight training bays, an Air Traffic Control (ATC) radar lab

CIS Aerospace Telemetry Lab where students log hours without leaving campus12.

Student Flight Bursary Endowment ($10.0M)

Impact: $400,000/Year in Perpetual Student Funding 13

What’s Included:

A $10M invested endowment (4% annual yield11) that cuts BBA Aviation student flight costs by ~40%

funds Indigenous youth aviation scholarships in partnership with the Stó:lō Nation

subsidises $25 Discovery Flights for any UFV student.

CIS FlightOps ERP & Electric Shuttles ($4.0M)

Connectivity: 10-Minute Campus-to-Tarmac Loop 13

What’s Included:

Agile development of the custom UFV FlightOps Software Suite (automated aircraft scheduling,

maintenance tracking

weather telemetry built with paid CIS co-op students) plus 2 dedicated Electric Shuttle Buses running every 20 minutes between UFV King Road and YXX.

Not in Scope

  • No Runway on King Road Main Campus: All live takeoffs and landings happen on existing YXX runways. Main campus is only for simulators, VR and ground school14
  • No Private Land Purchases: Ground Lease is the better choice after comparing prices
  • No Commercial Passenger Airline Operations: Strictly certified for collegiate flight instruction, research, and student discovery flights - No Heavy Jets or Military Aircraft: Fleet is capped at single engine, light twin-engine, and electric trainers suited for commercial licensing
  • No Post-2030 Operating Subsidies: Once the project starts on May 1,2030 , ongoing operations are 100% self-funded via student fight tuition, simulator rentals, and the $10M endowment yield
